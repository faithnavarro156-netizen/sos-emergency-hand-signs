Humanitarian & Civic Value

Bridging Communication Barriers When Every Second Counts

Traditional emergency response relies heavily on spoken calls and audible alarms. However, crises frequently occur where speech is impossible, unsafe, or unheard across linguistic and physical barriers.

By translating universal hand signals into instant machine-readable alerts, SOS.AI fosters inclusive community safety across schools, workplaces, public transit hubs, and disaster response zones.

Deaf & Nonverbal Accessibility

Empowers Deaf, hard-of-hearing, and speech-impaired individuals to communicate urgent distress or safety status visually without verbal barriers.

Discreet Silent Distress

Enables silent signaling during domestic emergencies, medical incapacitation, or security threats where speaking aloud could escalate danger.

Language-Independent & High-Noise

Bridges spoken language barriers during international disasters and overcomes acoustic interference on construction sites, factories, and crowded venues.

Zero-Cloud Privacy First

All neural inference runs locally inside the user's browser via WebGL/TensorFlow.js—no webcam footage is ever recorded or transmitted externally.

System Pipeline

How SOS.AI Works

Four streamlined stages from optical capture to actionable emergency telemetry.

STEP 01

Video / Image Capture

Captures live video frames from your device webcam or loads an uploaded static image directly into an HTML5 canvas buffer.

STEP 02

Edge TensorFlow.js Processing

TensorFlow.js processes each frame locally using your Teachable Machine MobileNet transfer-learning model (OdyQhhMXg).

STEP 03

Class Probability Scoring

Computes normalized softmax confidence percentages across all four trained emergency classes: HELP, STOP, ATTENTION, and SAFE.

STEP 04

Instant Visual Alerting

Triggers color-coded telemetry banners, live progress gauges, event logs, and gesture protocol highlights for immediate situational awareness.
